# AI Fraud Investigation Agent
**Autonomous AI-Powered Fraud Detection & Public Records Intelligence**

An AI-powered autonomous investigation agent designed to identify potential fraud and regulatory inconsistencies in childcare facilities across Illinois.

The solution cross-references Illinois DCFS licensing records, Cook County property information, Google Maps and Street View evidence, and Illinois Secretary of State business registrations.

Its investigation workflow identifies discrepancies between declared childcare capacity, physical infrastructure, business identity and publicly available evidence.

### Key Capabilities

- **Licensing Intelligence:** Search licensed childcare providers by ZIP code through Illinois DCFS records.
- **Geospatial Verification:** Cross-reference licensed capacity with Cook County building and property information.
- **Occupancy Analysis:** Calculate preliminary occupancy indicators using applicable Illinois childcare space requirements.
- **Visual Intelligence:** Evaluate Street View imagery for potential inconsistencies in facility appearance and location.
- **Business Verification:** Cross-reference Google Places information and Illinois Secretary of State registrations.
- **Pattern Detection:** Identify shared addresses, related business entities and suspicious cross-provider patterns.
- **Explainable Investigation:** Produce an auditable record of verification steps, findings and evidence for human review.

**Objective:** Transform fragmented public information into actionable investigative insights, reducing manual research and enabling more efficient, evidence-based fraud risk assessments.

*All automated findings are preliminary risk signals, not definitive determinations of fraud.*
